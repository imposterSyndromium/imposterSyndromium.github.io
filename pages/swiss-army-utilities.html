Frequently Asked Questions

What is Swiss Army Utilities?

Swiss Army Utilities is a comprehensive iOS app that combines multiple utility tools in one place. It includes unit converters, QR scanners, accessibility tools, navigation aids, and more - all designed for everyday convenience.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes! Privacy is our top priority. All sensitive data is encrypted locally using AES-256 encryption. We don't collect, track, or sync your data to any cloud services. Everything stays on your device.

Is the app accessible?

Absolutely! Swiss Army Utilities was built with accessibility as a core requirement. It includes full VoiceOver support, haptic feedback, Dynamic Type support, and tools specifically designed for users with visual or hearing impairments.

What iOS version do I need?

Swiss Army Utilities requires iOS 16.0 or later. It's optimized for the latest iPhone and iPad models, with adaptive layouts that work great on both device types.

Does the app work offline?

Yes! All utilities work completely offline. No internet connection is required for any of the core features. Your data is stored locally on your device.

How much does it cost?

Swiss Army Utilities will be available on the App Store. Pricing information will be announced closer to release. Check back soon for updates!

Can I request a new utility?

We'd love to hear your ideas! Send us a feature request via email, and we'll consider it for future updates. User feedback is invaluable in shaping the app's development.

How do I report a bug?

If you encounter any issues, please email us with details about the problem, your device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce. We'll investigate and fix it as soon as possible.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

Swiss Army Utilities ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Swiss Army Utilities operates entirely offline and does not connect to any external servers or services.

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No data collection or transmission
  • No third-party services or SDKs that collect data

Local Data Storage

All data created within the app (such as voice memos, notes, saved locations, etc.) is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in data storage. This data is:

  • Encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Stored only on your device
  • Never transmitted to external servers
  • Never shared with third parties

Permissions

Swiss Army Utilities may request the following permissions to provide functionality:

  • Camera: Used for QR code scanning, text recognition, color identification, and scene description features
  • Microphone: Used for voice memos, live speech-to-text, and sound alert detection
  • Location: Used for navigation tools, breadcrumb trail, and location-tagged voice memos
  • Photo Library: Used for saving images and accessing photos for analysis
  • Notifications: Used for reminders and alerts (optional)

All permissions are optional and can be denied. The app will continue to function with reduced capabilities if permissions are not granted.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your local data:

  • AES-256 encryption for sensitive data
  • iOS Keychain for secure credential storage
  • Local-only data storage (no cloud sync)
  • No network connectivity required

Children's Privacy

Swiss Army Utilities is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
