Inner Whisper

Words to return to when you need them.

Inner Whisper is a private, offline-first collection of meaningful quotes. Save favourites, shape the experience with themes and reminders, and add personal words with on-device protection—without creating an account.

  • Curated quote library and favourites
  • Custom themes and thoughtful reminders
  • Personal entries protected on device
  • Designed to work offline

Inner Thought

One gesture. Say what's inside. Keep it safely.

Inner Thought is a privacy-focused capture app for the thought that's about to disappear. Record it by voice, text, photo, or location — encrypted on-device, no account, nothing to organize in the moment.

  • Voice recording with transcription
  • Text and dictation capture
  • Photo, video, and location capture
  • On-device encryption, no account needed
